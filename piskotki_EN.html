cookie policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Your browser's local storage (localStorage) works in a similar way. Below we describe which ones the Mat.Kom, splošna mehanična dela, d.o.o. website uses and how you can manage your choice.

We use cookies in accordance with the Slovenian Electronic Communications Act (ZEKom-2) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Strictly necessary cookies do not require consent; all others are only set once you allow them.

1. Strictly necessary

The website cannot work properly without these. They do not require consent.

3. External resources without cookies

Fonts (Google Fonts), icons and scripts are loaded from Google, jsDelivr and unpkg servers. These do not set cookies, but they receive your IP address when loading, which is technically necessary to deliver the content.

4. Analytics and advertising

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this website.
