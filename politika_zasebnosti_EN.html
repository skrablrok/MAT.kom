privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

At Mat.Kom, splošna mehanična dela, d.o.o. we respect your privacy. This policy explains which personal data we process when you visit our website or contact us, why we process it and what rights you have. We process data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Slovenian Personal Data Protection Act (ZVOP-2).

2. What data we process and why

Enquiries (contact form, email, phone)

  • Data: first and last name, email address, phone number, subject, message content and attached files.
  • Purpose: replying to your enquiry, preparing quotes and business communication.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in replying to messages we receive (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: up to 2 years after the last communication. If we enter into a contract, we keep the data for as long as required by law (e.g. accounting and tax regulations).

Job applications

  • Data: first and last name, email address, address, city, postcode, date and your CV with any other data you send us.
  • Purpose: carrying out the candidate selection process.
  • Legal basis: steps taken before entering into an employment contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). If you would like us to keep your application for future vacancies, we need your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Retention: until the selection process is complete, after which the data is deleted. With your consent, we keep it for up to 1 year.

Visiting the website

  • Data: IP address, browser and device type, time of visit and pages visited, logged automatically by our hosting provider's server.
  • Purpose: keeping the website running and secure.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: according to the hosting provider's rules, usually a short period.

We do not use the website for advertising or visitor analytics, and we do not sell personal data.

3. Processors and recipients

Data may be processed by external providers that help us run the website, only to the extent needed for their service:

  • our web hosting provider (the server the website runs on),
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards web form submissions to our email address,
  • Google Ireland Ltd. for displaying Google Maps (only with your consent) and Google Fonts,
  • jsDelivr and unpkg (content delivery networks) that serve icons and scripts.

Some of these providers may process data (mainly your IP address) outside the European Economic Area, for example in the USA. Such transfers are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

We may also disclose data to public authorities where required by law.

4. Your rights

Regarding your personal data, you have the right to:

  • access your data and obtain a copy,
  • have inaccurate data corrected,
  • have your data erased ("right to be forgotten"),
  • restrict processing,
  • data portability,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Send your request to c.info@matkom.si. We will reply within one month at the latest.

If you believe the processing of your data breaches the law, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia, Dunajska cesta 22, 1000 Ljubljana, www.ip-rs.si, or with the supervisory authority in your country of residence.

5. Data security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Only people who need the data for their work have access to it.

6. Cookies

How we use cookies is described in our cookie policy.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page, with the date of the last change shown at the top.
